1 m roadmap · v = 1 m/s · one node per 1 s tick

Safety rule: robot A enters node m only if

✓
A claimed m in the previous round
✓
every peer has acknowledged hearing A recently
✓
no fresh peer is at m or claims m, and m is outside every stale peer's reach set
Lost packet ⇒ wait, never collide
= collision avoidance by construction
